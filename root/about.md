## Format

- The event will follow standard 2027 BIOBUZZ season format, with a double elimination bracket (subject to change).
- There will be qualification matches, alliance selection, eliminations, and awards at the end (see [schedule](#schedule) for more details).

## Applications

- The 2027 [application](/apply) deadline will be announced soon. Applications will be reviewed and selected on a rolling basis.
- Accepted teams will receive information regarding how to pay for registration via email.

## Payment

- Payment is $50 USD for accepted teams.
- Additionally, you can pay increments of $35 to compete with extra robots, if there is enough space.
- We will **never** ask you for your credit card information on Discord, email, phone call, or other means - all payments will be made through a secure payment processor.
