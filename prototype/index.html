Washington's oldest FTC offseason competition.

West Coast Invitational is an annual offseason FIRST Tech Challenge competition, built so teams can show their full potential and everything they pulled off this season, at an event that's fun first and still plenty competitive.

Format
Standard BIOBUZZ season format: qualification matches, alliance selection, double-elimination playoffs, awards.
Fields
One competition field, plus a practice field.
Stream
Every match live on the WCI YouTube channel, with replays after.
Awards
Trophies for winning and finalist alliance captains and partners, plus the WCI Board Award for one truly outstanding team. More awards announcing soon.

The road to Tacoma

Applications are open now and reviewed on a rolling basis. The deadline is announcing soon, so don't wait.

  1. Apply

    Fill out the team application. We review as they come in.

    Open the application

  2. Get accepted

    Accepted teams get an email with registration and payment details.

  3. Pay

    $50 per team. Extra robots are $35 each if there's space. Payments only go through a secure processor; we will never ask for card details over Discord, email, or phone.

    Refund policy announcing soon

  4. Read the team packet

    Pit sizes, what to bring and leave at home, load-in, and judging.

    Team packet announcing soon

  5. Load in

    Park on site at Foss. Check-in runs 8:30–9:30 AM, robot inspection 9:00–10:30 AM.

  6. Compete

    Quals, alliance selection, playoffs, awards. Then do it all again next year.
